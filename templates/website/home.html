About Sri Sabari Yogashala

Rooted in Tradition.
Inspired by Purpose.
Open to the World.

Sri Sabari Yogashala was founded by Sunila and Arun, a husband-and-wife team whose personal journey with yoga inspired a shared purpose — to share its positive and transformative essence with others.

Located in the peaceful coastal town of Varkala, Kerala, we welcome students from India and around the world to experience authentic yoga in a calm, supportive and traditional environment.

Sri Sabari Yogashala is a Yoga Alliance Registered Yoga School (RYS 200), committed to delivering authentic teacher training while maintaining internationally recognised educational standards.

Our curriculum combines traditional yogic wisdom with structured teacher training, helping students develop the confidence, knowledge and practical skills required to begin their teaching journey.
