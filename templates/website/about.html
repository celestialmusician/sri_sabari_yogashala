About Sri Sabari Yogashala

Rooted In Tradition.
Inspired By Purpose.

Sri Sabari Yogashala was founded by Sunila and Arun, a husband-and-wife team whose personal journey with yoga inspired a shared purpose—to share its positive and transformative essence with others.

Located in the peaceful coastal town of Varkala, Kerala, our Yoga Alliance Registered Yoga School welcomes students from India and around the world to experience authentic yoga in a supportive and traditional learning environment.

Beyond teacher training, our vision is to make yoga accessible to everyone while preserving its traditional values and encouraging mindful, balanced living.

Meet Our Founders

Sunila & Arun

Sri Sabari Yogashala was founded by Sunila and Arun, whose shared journey through yoga inspired a deeper purpose—to create a space where authentic yoga could be learned, experienced and shared with people from around the world.

What began as a personal practice soon evolved into a vision of building a welcoming yoga school rooted in traditional values, mindful living and holistic wellbeing.

Today, they continue to guide students with sincerity, compassion and a commitment to preserving the timeless wisdom of yoga while making it accessible to modern practitioners.
