About Sri Sabari Yogashala

Rooted In Tradition.
Inspired By Purpose.

Sri Sabari Yogashala is a premier yoga school dedicated to sharing the positive and transformative essence of traditional yoga with students from around the world.

Located in the peaceful coastal town of Varkala, Kerala, our Yoga Alliance Registered Yoga School welcomes students from India and around the world to experience authentic yoga in a supportive and traditional learning environment.

Beyond teacher training, our vision is to make yoga accessible to everyone while preserving its traditional values and encouraging mindful, balanced living.

Our Purpose

Vision & Philosophy

Our Story

Every meaningful journey begins with a purpose. Sri Sabari Yogashala was established with a sincere vision to share the authentic wisdom of traditional yoga in a peaceful and supportive learning environment. Our goal is to bring together students from different cultures and backgrounds, creating a community where learning, self-discovery, and personal transformation naturally flourish. We believe yoga is more than physical practice—it is a complete way of living that nurtures the body, mind, and spirit.

Our Vision

To become one of Kerala’s most respected international yoga schools by offering authentic traditional yoga education with integrity, compassion, and excellence. We aspire to build a global yoga community where students leave not only with internationally recognized training but also with greater confidence, inner peace, and a lifelong connection to yoga.

Destination Varkala

WHY VARKALA?

Varkala is one of India’s most inspiring yoga destinations.

Its spectacular cliffs, golden beaches, tropical landscape, breathtaking sunsets, and peaceful atmosphere create the perfect setting for yoga, meditation, and self-discovery.

Here, every sunrise inspires a new beginning, every breath feels deeper, and every practice becomes more meaningful.

At Sri Sabari Yogashala, nature itself becomes part of your learning journey.
