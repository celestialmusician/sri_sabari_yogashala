Course Overview

Build A Strong Foundation In Traditional Yoga

The 200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training at Sri Sabari Yogashala is a comprehensive Yoga Alliance–aligned program designed for aspiring teachers and dedicated practitioners. Through traditional teachings, disciplined practice, and experienced guidance, students develop the confidence to teach yoga safely while deepening their own personal journey.

The course blends classical Hatha Yoga, Ashtanga Vinyasa, Pranayama, Meditation, Anatomy, Yoga Philosophy, and practical teaching methodology within the peaceful environment of Varkala, Kerala.
