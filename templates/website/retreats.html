Why Varkala?

Discover Kerala's Most Inspiring Yoga Destination

Varkala is one of India's most loved wellness destinations, where dramatic sea cliffs meet golden beaches, lush palm groves and a peaceful spiritual atmosphere. It is the perfect place to disconnect from daily stress and reconnect with yourself.

From breathtaking sunsets over the Arabian Sea to authentic Ayurveda, welcoming cafés and vibrant local culture, Varkala offers an unforgettable experience beyond the yoga mat.
